The first release of PhoneOn, a free, private app that keeps your Android screen on for as long as you choose.

- Timers of 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours or until you stop it, all changeable, plus +15 minutes
- A quiet notification that only shows in the pull-down panel, with Stop and +15 min
- Three home-screen widgets (switch, status, timers) and a pull-down tile
- Stops by itself when the timer ends, at low battery and when you press the power button
- Optional auto modes: keep the screen on while chosen apps are open, or while charging
- Optional floating dot, light, dark, match-phone and wallpaper-colour themes
- Never changes your phone's own screen timeout and never asks for "Modify system settings"
- No adverts, no account and no internet access

Needs Android 8 or later. Download `PhoneOn.apk` below and open it to install.
