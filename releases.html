Release notes

What changed in each version of PhoneOn. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.0 26 November 2026Latest

  • First release. Keeps your screen on for as long as you choose, with no internet access.
  • Timers of 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours or until you stop it, all changeable, plus +15 minutes.
  • A quiet notification that only shows in the pull-down panel, with Stop and +15 min. It comes back if swiped away.
  • Three home-screen widgets (switch, status, timers) and a pull-down tile.
  • Stops by itself when the timer ends, at low battery (15% by default) and when you press the power button.
  • Optional auto modes: keep the screen on while chosen apps are open, or while charging.
  • Optional floating dot, vibration and pop-up messages, light, dark, match-phone and wallpaper-colour themes.
  • Never changes your phone's own screen timeout and never asks for "Modify system settings".

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.0 is called PhoneOn.apk. It is 1.3 MB (1,348,331 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

44efd1d1e937c93c31644c951caff2a13d20accb8bad878969af88a2b93e8b3b

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile PhoneOn.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps your settings.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
