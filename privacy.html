Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. This policy covers the PhoneOn app for Android (package uk.jj.phoneon) and this website. PhoneOn is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who can be reached by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.

The short version

  • PhoneOn does not hold Android's internet permission. It cannot go online, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • It keeps only your settings, on your phone.
  • There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your phone

PhoneOn keeps your settings, such as your timers, theme and chosen apps, in the app's private storage. Android's cloud backup and device transfer are switched off for PhoneOn.

The list of appsChoosing apps for the chosen-apps auto modeOnly when you open Choose apps

What it never does

PhoneOn never asks for "Modify system settings" and never changes your phone's screen timeout. The 1-pixel layer cannot see or touch what other apps show. Usage access tells it only which app is open, never what is in it.

Links to this website

Settings, About has Check for updates and More apps. Each opens this site in your browser; PhoneOn itself never goes online.

Keeping and deleting your data

Uninstalling PhoneOn deletes everything it holds.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no scripts beyond the app list. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on GitHub. This policy was last updated on 26 November 2026.
